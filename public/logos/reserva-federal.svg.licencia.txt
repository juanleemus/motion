|description=Seal of the United States {{W|Federal Reserve System}}.
The seal has most of the elements of the {{F|US-FederalReserveBoard-Seal.svg|Board of Governors seal}}.  A version is printed on all U.S. {{W|Federal Reserve Note}}s redesigned since 1996 (replacing the letter of the bank which printed the note, which was used in earlier designs).
